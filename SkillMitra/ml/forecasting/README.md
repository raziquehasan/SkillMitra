# Forecasting Module

## Purpose

Forecast future skill demand based on historical labour market signals and sector trends.

## Planned Approach

- Time-series modeling on skill demand signals
- Sector-specific growth projection inputs
- Short-term (6–12 month) and medium-term (2–3 year) demand forecasts
- District-level forecast aggregation for training planning

## Inputs

- Historical skill demand time series (from demand_analysis module)
- Sector growth indicators
- Technology adoption signals

## Outputs

- Skill demand forecast curves per skill-location pair
- High-growth skill alerts
- Obsolescence risk scores for skills and courses

## Libraries

- `scikit-learn`
- `pandas`, `numpy`
