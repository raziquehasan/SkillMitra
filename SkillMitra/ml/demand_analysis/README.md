# Demand Analysis Module

## Purpose

Analyze aggregated labour market signals to produce structured skill demand intelligence
by role, skill, location, and proficiency level.

## Planned Approach

- Aggregate signals: job postings, employer surveys, sector growth data, placement outcomes
- Compute demand scores per skill-role-location combination
- Identify demand trends over time
- Detect emerging skills and declining skill areas
- Produce district-level demand breakdowns

## Inputs

- Processed job posting data
- Employer survey responses
- Sector growth indicators
- Placement and outcome records

## Outputs

- Skill demand matrix (skill × location × role × time)
- Top-N skills by demand per district/sector
- Emerging skill signals
- Demand trend time series

## Libraries

- `pandas`, `numpy`
- `scikit-learn`
